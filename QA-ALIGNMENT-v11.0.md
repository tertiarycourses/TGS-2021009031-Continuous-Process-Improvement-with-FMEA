## Case and activity traceability

All activities use the same Orion Precision Electronics SMT process and advance one evidence chain:

1. CI charter and governance.
2. SIPOC, process scope and PDCA plan.
3. Pareto and stratification.
4. Fishbone, 5 Whys, Fault Tree and cause verification.
5. Process FMEA construction.
6. S/O/D rating, action planning and re-scoring.
7. Weighted decision, Pugh and cost-benefit selection.
8. Pilot, change control and coaching.
9. Stability, capability and SPC interpretation.
10. Process Control Plan and review loop.
