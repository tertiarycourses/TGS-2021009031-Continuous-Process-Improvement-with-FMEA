# Activity 10 — Create the Process Control Plan and Review Loop

**Course:** Continuous Process Improvement with FMEA (TGS-2021009031)  
**Assessment mapping:** A9, A10, K4  
**Objective:** Translate PFMEA risks into operational controls, reaction plans and FMEA review triggers.

## Scenario

Orion must hand the improved SMT process back to production without losing the risk knowledge created by the team.

## Deliverable

Process Control Plan, reaction plan, audit checklist, control-to-PFMEA trace matrix and review calendar

## Files

- `Starter.xlsx` — your working file.
- `Completed.xlsx` — a worked comparison; review after attempting the activity.
- `Activity-Brief.pdf` — printable scenario, questions and submission check.
- `Checklist.md` — final verification.

## Detailed procedure

### Step 1 — Select the critical process/product characteristics from high-priority PFMEA rows and the pilot evidence.

Open or update: **Orion-Control-Plan.xlsx · Traceability**.

Record your rationale, source evidence and any assumption directly in the workbook. Keep the entry specific enough for another team member to reproduce your decision.

### Step 2 — For each characteristic, state specification/target, method, sample size, frequency, owner and record.

Open or update: **Orion-Control-Plan.xlsx · Control Plan**.

Record your rationale, source evidence and any assumption directly in the workbook. Keep the entry specific enough for another team member to reproduce your decision.

### Step 3 — Write a reaction plan covering stop/contain, suspect-product boundary, notification, correction, verification and release.

Open or update: **Orion-Control-Plan.xlsx · Reaction Plan**.

Record your rationale, source evidence and any assumption directly in the workbook. Keep the entry specific enough for another team member to reproduce your decision.

### Step 4 — Check the measurement method, training and escalation are practical for all shifts.

Record your rationale, source evidence and any assumption directly in the workbook. Keep the entry specific enough for another team member to reproduce your decision.

### Step 5 — Link each control row to PFMEA row ID and identify whether it prevents a cause or detects cause/mode.
