# Activity 07 — Select the Improvement Portfolio

**Course:** Continuous Process Improvement with FMEA (TGS-2021009031)  
**Assessment mapping:** A7, A8, K2  
**Objective:** Identify and compare risk-reduction opportunities using transparent criteria.

## Scenario

Orion can fund only two of five proposed improvements during the next production quarter.

## Deliverable

Opportunity register, weighted decision matrix, Pugh matrix and cost-benefit summary

## Files

- `Starter.xlsx` — your working file.
- `Completed.xlsx` — a worked comparison; review after attempting the activity.
- `Activity-Brief.pdf` — printable scenario, questions and submission check.
- `Checklist.md` — final verification.

## Detailed procedure

### Step 1 — Translate PFMEA actions into five distinct options that address a named failure mechanism.

Open or update: **Improvement-Selection.xlsx · Options**.

Record your rationale, source evidence and any assumption directly in the workbook. Keep the entry specific enough for another team member to reproduce your decision.

### Step 2 — Agree weights for risk reduction, effectiveness, feasibility, speed, cost, side effects and sustainability.

Open or update: **Improvement-Selection.xlsx · Criteria**.

Record your rationale, source evidence and any assumption directly in the workbook. Keep the entry specific enough for another team member to reproduce your decision.

### Step 3 — Score every option with evidence notes; calculate weighted totals using formulas.

Open or update: **Improvement-Selection.xlsx · Weighted Matrix**.

Record your rationale, source evidence and any assumption directly in the workbook. Keep the entry specific enough for another team member to reproduce your decision.

### Step 4 — Choose the current method as the Pugh datum and compare each option as better, same or worse by criterion.

Open or update: **Improvement-Selection.xlsx · Pugh**.

Record your rationale, source evidence and any assumption directly in the workbook. Keep the entry specific enough for another team member to reproduce your decision.

### Step 5 — Estimate benefit and cost as ranges, including validation, training and maintenance—not only purchase cost.

Open or update: **Improvement-Selection.xlsx · Cost Benefit**.

Record your rationale, source evidence and any assumption directly in the workbook. Keep the entry specific enough for another team member to reproduce your decision.

### Step 6 — Run a weight sensitivity check and recommend two actions with risks, dependencies and assumptions.

Open or update: **Improvement-Selection.xlsx · Recommendation**.

Record your rationale, source evidence and any assumption directly in the workbook. Keep the entry specific enough for another team member to reproduce your decision.

## Scenario questions

1. Does the top score remain top when cost weight doubles?
2. Which option prevents the cause rather than merely detecting the defect?

## Acceptance criterion

The recommendation traces to PFMEA risks, calculations are formula-driven, assumptions are visible and trade-offs are explicitly discussed.
