# Activity 06 — Rate Risk, Plan Actions and Re-score

**Course:** Continuous Process Improvement with FMEA (TGS-2021009031)  
**Assessment mapping:** A5, A6, K3  
**Objective:** Prioritise PFMEA actions using severity-first judgement, RPN context and verified re-rating.

## Scenario

Several Orion rows have similar RPNs but different customer and safety consequences.

## Deliverable

Rated PFMEA with RPN formulas, internal action-priority guardrail, owners, due dates and post-action scores

## Files

- `Starter.xlsx` — your working file.
- `Completed.xlsx` — a worked comparison; review after attempting the activity.
- `Activity-Brief.pdf` — printable scenario, questions and submission check.
- `Checklist.md` — final verification.

## Scenario questions

1. Why can an RPN of 40 require more urgent action than an RPN of 108?
2. Which rating should change after adding only a better inspection?

## Acceptance criterion

All RPN formulas are correct, severe risks remain visible, actions have ownership/evidence, and post-action ratings match the control change.
