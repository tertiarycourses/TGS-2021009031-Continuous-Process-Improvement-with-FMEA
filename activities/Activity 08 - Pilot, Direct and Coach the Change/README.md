**Course:** Continuous Process Improvement with FMEA (TGS-2021009031)

1. What would cause the pilot to stop immediately?
2. How will the coach distinguish non-adoption from an ineffective countermeasure?

## Acceptance criterion

The pilot is measurable, contained and reversible; the change has approvals, training and a documented scale/revise/stop rule.
