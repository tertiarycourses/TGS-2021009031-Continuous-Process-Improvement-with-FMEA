# Activity 01 — Launch the Orion Continuous Improvement System

**Course:** Continuous Process Improvement with FMEA (TGS-2021009031)  
**Assessment mapping:** A1, A2, K1  
**Objective:** Create governance, coaching and visible management for the improvement effort.

## Scenario

Orion's SMT line has competing priorities, informal escalation and no shared definition of success.

## Deliverable

CI charter, role map, coaching cadence and daily/weekly review board

## Files

- `Starter.xlsx` — your working file.
- `Completed.xlsx` — a worked comparison; review after attempting the activity.

## Scenario questions

1. Which decision belongs to the sponsor rather than the team?
2. How will the process owner know the improvement is sustained?

## Acceptance criterion
